Privacy Policy

InAndOut - Your Declutter Tracker

Last updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Your privacy matters to us. This privacy policy explains how the InAndOut app handles your data.

In short: InAndOut does not collect any personal data and does not send any data to external servers.

2. What Data is Stored?

InAndOut only stores data that you enter yourself:

  • Number of items added and decluttered
  • Optional notes for entries
  • Your goals, streaks, and progress
  • Rooms and areas (Locations feature)
  • App settings (language, reminders)

3. Where is Data Stored?

Locally on your device: All data is stored locally within the app (UserDefaults).

Optional: iCloud Sync: If you enable iCloud synchronization, your data is synced through your personal iCloud account. This allows usage across multiple devices. Apple manages this data according to Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Data Collection

InAndOut:

  • Does not use analytics or tracking tools
  • Does not display advertisements
  • Does not send data to external servers
  • Does not share data with third parties

5. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app uses local notifications on your device. This requires notification permissions. No data is sent to push notification servers.

6. Your Rights

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Access: All data is visible directly in the app
  • Deletion: You can reset all data in Settings
  • Export: Your data stays on your device or in your iCloud

7. Changes

If there are significant changes to this privacy policy, the app will be updated. The current version is always available here.
