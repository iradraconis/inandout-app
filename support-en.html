Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does InAndOut work?

Track everything that comes into your household (In) and what you declutter (Out). The goal is to declutter more than you add - creating lasting order!

Is my data synchronized?

You can optionally enable iCloud sync in Settings. This makes your data available across all your Apple devices.

Can I delete my data?

Yes! In Settings you'll find the option "Reset all data".

What are Challenges?

Challenges are time-limited goals with milestones that motivate you to declutter regularly. Choose from 15 different challenges!

What is the Locations feature?

With the Locations feature, you can divide your household into rooms and areas, and systematically declutter room by room.
